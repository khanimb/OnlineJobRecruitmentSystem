Privacy Policy

NexHire collects only the information you provide when creating an account, posting jobs, or applying to positions — such as your name, email, CV, and profile details.

Your data is used solely to operate the platform: matching candidates with jobs, processing applications, and enabling communication between employers and job seekers. We do not sell your data to third parties.

Payment information is processed securely through Stripe and is never stored on our servers.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.
